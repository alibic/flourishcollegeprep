How many sessions will my student need?

Moderate Improvement

Aiming for 50–100 SAT points or 2–3 ACT points? Somewhere between 4–6 sessions is typically sufficient.

Significant Improvement

Targeting 300+ SAT points or 8+ ACT points? Expect a few months and 15–20 sessions — it’s absolutely achievable with the right investment of time and effort.

A strong test score can open real doors — including merit-based scholarships. The University of Maine, for example, offers a full-ride merit scholarship, and strong scores help students stand out.

At Flourish, we take all factors into consideration. Please don’t hesitate to share your financial limitations — we’ll build a plan that works for your family. Once you’ve worked with us, we’re always available. If tutoring becomes unaffordable, we’re happy to provide resources so students can keep preparing independently.
