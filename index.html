Our Mission

Tutoring with a different vision

Flourish College Prep was created out of a vision of tutoring students differently. Our primary goal is to teach students how to learn — not just to fill academic gaps, but to equip kids to fill those gaps independently.

We believe the best tutoring leads to less tutoring. Our goal is to give every student the tools, strategies, and confidence to face challenges on their own terms.

Independence First

Our goal is to transition every student to independence as quickly as possible. Tutoring should be a resource, not a crutch.

Process Over Outcome

We teach students how to think, not just what to think. The pathway to the best outcomes runs through process.

Accessible to All

We take every family's situation into consideration. Sliding scale pricing ensures every student can get the support they need.

Our Services

Test Prep

ACT / SAT Prep

The ACT and SAT are often the first big hurdles in college admissions — and they can feel overwhelming. Our goal is to help your student gain real confidence, not just improve a number on a page.

We teach students to think about the tests like a game, and equip them with strategies to win it. Our specialized materials cover all test content, and we work with each student to build a personalized approach around their unique strengths and weaknesses.

Who We Are

Meet the Team

Caiden
Co-Founder · Tutor

Caiden has been a full-time tutor for over a decade. After graduating from Cornell University with a degree in English and Philosophy, she worked at the Windward School for students with language-based learning disabilities. While she loved teaching, she struggled when she had to move on from a concept before all students had reached full understanding. When she discovered tutoring, she knew she had found her perfect teaching fit.

Caiden specializes in test prep, college essays, English, math, and study skills. She has helped students improve their scores by over 400 points on the SAT and up to 14 points on the ACT. But she is most gratified when students tell her they've gained overall confidence — or when they apply skills learned in tutoring to other areas of their life. It's not uncommon for Caiden to guide students through every step of the college prep process, from applications to school selection.

Caiden lives in South Portland with her dog Buster. She loves Russian literature, hiking in the Maine woods, writing poetry, and making (very bad) collages!

Ali
Co-Founder · Tutor

Ali brings over a decade of hands-on experience in applied math and science, giving her a unique ability to connect school concepts to real-world applications. She holds a bachelor's in Chemistry from the University of Wisconsin and a master's in Data Analytics from Southern New Hampshire University, and has worked across industries as a chemical engineer, business intelligence analyst, data engineer, and data analytics director. This interdisciplinary background means she can offer students not just academic support, but genuine insight into where their education can take them.

Ali has helped students improve their grades by multiple letter grades across a range of subjects, specializing in high school sciences and mathematics, with experience ranging into post-graduate business courses. She also offers SAT/ACT tutoring, drawing on the extensive strategies of her co-founder Caiden, who first recognized Ali's natural teaching ability and brought her into tutoring several years ago.

In her free time, Ali is often running after her two daughters, dogs, cats, and chickens, or checking in on her bees. She also enjoys drawing, knitting, reading, and tennis.
