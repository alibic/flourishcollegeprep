Our Mission

Tutoring with a different vision

Flourish College Prep was created out of a vision of tutoring students differently. Our primary goal at Flourish is to teach students how to learn. We have extensive experience to help fill academic gaps, but our program is meant to teach kids how to fill gaps in their knowledge independently.

We believe the best tutoring leads to less tutoring — equipping students with the tools, strategies, and confidence to face challenges on their own terms.

Independence First

Our goal is to transition every student to independence as quickly as possible. Tutoring should be a resource, not a crutch.

Process Over Outcome

We've found the pathway to the best outcomes is through process — teaching students how to think, not just what to think.

Accessible to All

We take every family's financial situation into consideration. Sliding scale pricing ensures every student can get the support they need.

Our Services

Test Prep

ACT / SAT Prep

At Flourish, we understand that the ACT/SAT process is one of the first hurdles in an often mystifying college admissions process. Our goal is to help your student gain confidence — not just on these tests, but in their ability to face a significant challenge as they prepare for the transition into college.

We teach students how to think about the tests like a game and equip them with strategies to win it. We've developed specialized materials covering test content, while also working with each student to individualize the process around their unique strengths and weaknesses.

Academic Support

Academic Tutoring

In addition to test prep, we offer tutoring in all main academic subjects, as well as college essay and application support. Our goal is to equip your child with study skills and confidence in order to transition them to independence as soon as possible.

Our philosophy is that tutoring is a resource — we want to be sure it does not turn into a crutch. We teach students how to find resources in themselves and their immediate environment so that they can become independent, self-reliant adults.

Ali brings over a decade of hands-on experience in applied math and science, giving her a unique ability to connect school concepts to real-world applications. How many sessions will my student need?

Moderate Improvement

Aiming for 50–100 points on the SAT or 2–3 points on the ACT? Somewhere between 4–6 sessions is typically sufficient.

Significant Improvement

Targeting 300+ SAT points or 8+ ACT points? Expect a few months and 15–20 sessions. These increases absolutely achievable with the right investment of time and effort.

A strong test score is an investment that can genuinely pay off — opening doors to merit-based scholarships. The University of Maine, for example, offers a full-ride merit scholarship, and strong scores help students stand out.

At Flourish, we take all factors into consideration. Don't hesitate to share your financial limitations — we will work with you to build a plan that fits both your student's goals and your family's needs.
